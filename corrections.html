Corrections log

Every correction made to London School Directory is recorded here — what was reported, what we changed, and when. Transparency about our data quality matters.

How this works

When a visitor emails us about an error, we verify it against the source data (DfE GIAS, Ofsted, or the relevant borough's admissions statistics), make the fix, and log it here. The log is updated manually after each correction.

Want to report something? Email us with the school name, postcode, and what's wrong.
