Corrections log

Every correction made to London School Directory is recorded here. If you spot an error, email us and we'll verify and fix it.

How corrections work

When a visitor reports an error, we verify it against the source — DfE GIAS, Ofsted, or the relevant borough's admissions data — make the fix, and log it here. We aim to respond within 5 working days.

If a school has recently changed its name, address, or Ofsted rating and the update isn't showing yet, please give it a week as source databases can lag. If it's still wrong after that, email us.

For school administrators

If you manage a school and want to update your details, the canonical place to do it is Get Information About Schools (GIAS). Once updated there, changes will flow through to our site on the next rebuild.

For Ofsted ratings, updates flow automatically once Ofsted publish the inspection report. If your rating is correct on Ofsted's site but wrong here, email us and we'll patch it manually.

Removing a school

If your school has closed, merged, or significantly changed its age range, please email us so we can remove or update the listing correctly.
