Privacy Policy

London School Directory is a free, independent website. We collect as little data as possible, use no advertising trackers, and never sell your information.

The short version No accounts. No login. No tracking cookies. No advertising. We don't sell or share your data. The only data we handle is privacy-respecting, anonymised analytics, plus a postcode lookup that happens when you search — described in full below.

This policy explains what data is processed when you use the London School Directory website (londonschool.directory) and the London Schools Directory Android app, which is simply a wrapper around the same website. It applies equally to both.

Who we are

London School Directory is built and maintained by Thierry, an independent developer based in London, and acts as the data controller for the limited processing described here. The site is hosted on Vercel and its source code is published openly on GitHub. You can contact us at Thierry@ThierryAmiot.com.

Information we process

1. Privacy-respecting analytics

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand which pages are popular and how fast they load. These are cookieless and collect data in aggregate and anonymised form — for example: the page visited, approximate region (country/region, not your precise location), device and browser type, referrer, and page-load performance metrics. This data is not used to identify you, is not sold, and is not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

2. Postcode and location search

When you type a postcode into the search box — or tap "use my location", which asks your browser for your device's location with your permission — that postcode or those coordinates are sent to Postcodes.io, a free UK postcode lookup service, to convert them into a location so we can show you nearby schools. This lookup happens in real time. We do not store your postcode or location on our servers, and it is not linked to any identity. "Use my location" is entirely optional; if you decline, you can simply type a postcode instead, or not search at all.

3. If you email us

If you email us (for a correction, question or partnership), we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of corrections. We do not add you to any marketing list.

Information we do not collect

  • No names, accounts, usernames or passwords
  • No payment or financial information
  • No advertising identifiers and no remarketing
  • No tracking cookies and no cross-site tracking
  • No selling or renting of personal data to anyone, ever

Third-party services

To display pages, some content is loaded from third parties. As a normal part of delivering web content, these providers may receive your IP address and basic request information. We do not control their processing; their own privacy policies apply.

Cookies

We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. Our analytics are cookieless. Your browser may store standard technical data (such as a service-worker cache that lets the app work offline), but this stays on your device and is not used to track you.

The Android app

The London Schools Directory Android app is a Trusted Web Activity — a thin wrapper that displays this website. It processes the same information in the same way as the website and collects nothing additional. It requires internet access to load pages. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone or camera. Location is only ever used if you choose "use my location" within the page, using your device's standard location permission, and is handled exactly as described above.

Children's privacy

The site is intended for parents, carers and other adults researching schools. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR) you have rights to access, correct, erase or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we hold essentially no personal data about visitors, most requests will simply confirm that we hold nothing that identifies you. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, contact us and we will remove it. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Data retention

Analytics data is retained by Vercel in aggregate, anonymised form only. Postcode and location lookups are not retained by us. Email correspondence is kept only as long as needed to handle your query and any related correction.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and its "last updated" date. Material changes will be noted on the page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: Thierry@ThierryAmiot.com. We aim to reply within 2 working days.
