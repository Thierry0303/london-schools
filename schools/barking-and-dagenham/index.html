Barking and Dagenham is one of East London's most affordable boroughs and has seen significant investment in its schools. The borough has a strong mix of primary and secondary provision, with several schools rated Outstanding by Ofsted. It borders Essex and offers good transport links into central London via the District and Overground lines.
