Barnet is one of London's highest-performing boroughs for education, with a large number of Outstanding and Good schools across both primary and secondary phases. The borough includes several selective grammar schools and is consistently popular with families relocating from central London. Areas such as Finchley, East Barnet and Hendon are particularly sought after for their school catchments.
