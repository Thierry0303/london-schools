Schools in Brent, London

Brent's schools cover Wembley, Kilburn, Willesden, Harlesden, Kingsbury and Neasden. The borough has a notable concentration of high-performing primaries and an improving secondary picture, with several schools among London's most oversubscribed. Admissions are coordinated by the council.

What the data says about Brent

82.4% of Brent schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Brent is 1.25, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Brent over the most recent reporting month is 147.44, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Brent's schools is 3.31 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Brent?

Brent has 102 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 70 are primary, 32 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Brent schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 15 schools in Brent (14.7%) are rated Outstanding, and 82.4% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Brent?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Brent's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Brent?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Brent appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Brent-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Brent schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
