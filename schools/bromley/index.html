Bromley is one of London's largest boroughs and benefits from a strong educational landscape with numerous Outstanding-rated schools. Like Bexley, it operates grammar schools alongside comprehensive and faith schools. The borough has a suburban feel with good green space and is popular with families seeking larger homes within commuting distance of central London.
