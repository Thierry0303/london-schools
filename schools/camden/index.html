Camden is an inner-London borough with a diverse and highly competitive school landscape. The borough is home to some of London's most oversubscribed primaries and highly regarded secondaries. Areas such as Hampstead, Primrose Hill and Gospel Oak attract families for their school catchments. Camden consistently invests in education and has a higher-than-average proportion of Outstanding schools.
