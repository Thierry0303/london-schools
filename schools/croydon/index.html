Croydon is south London's largest borough and has one of the most varied school landscapes in the capital, ranging from Outstanding independents to schools requiring improvement. The borough is undergoing significant regeneration and has seen investment in its educational infrastructure. Selective and faith school options are available alongside a full range of comprehensive schools.
