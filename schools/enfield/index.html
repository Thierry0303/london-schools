Enfield is a north London borough bordering Hertfordshire with a wide range of school types including grammar, faith, academy and independent schools. The borough spans leafy suburban areas in the north such as Winchmore Hill and Palmers Green, through to more urban areas in the south. Several schools consistently achieve above national average exam results.
