Hackney is one of East London's most dynamic boroughs and has transformed its educational offer over the past decade. The borough now has one of the highest proportions of Outstanding-rated schools in inner London. Highly regarded schools such as Gayhurst Community School and Queensbridge Primary make it a competitive catchment. Secondary provision includes Stoke Newington School and Mossbourne Community Academy.
