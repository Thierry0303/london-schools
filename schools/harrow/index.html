Harrow is a north-west London borough with a diverse and high-performing school system. The borough is home to the renowned independent Harrow School as well as a strong state sector. Many schools serve large South Asian communities and have a strong ethos around academic achievement. Harrow-on-the-Hill and Pinner are particularly popular areas for families.
