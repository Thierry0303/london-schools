Schools in Harrow, London

Harrow's schools cover the borough from Stanmore and Pinner through to Harrow town and Wealdstone. The borough has a strong reputation for academic performance and a notable faith-school presence. 11+ preparation is widespread among Year 4 and Year 5 families.

What the data says about Harrow

82.2% of Harrow schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Harrow is 1.36, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Harrow over the most recent reporting month is 84.67, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Harrow's schools is 5.68 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Harrow?

Harrow has 73 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 51 are primary, 23 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Harrow schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 17 schools in Harrow (23.3%) are rated Outstanding, and 82.2% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Harrow?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Harrow's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Harrow?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Harrow appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Harrow-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Harrow schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
