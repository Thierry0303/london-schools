Havering is the easternmost London borough, bordering Essex, and has a strong tradition of selective education with grammar schools including Royal Liberty and Coopers' Company and Coborn. The borough has a largely suburban character with lower house prices than inner London, making it attractive to families seeking larger homes and strong school options.
