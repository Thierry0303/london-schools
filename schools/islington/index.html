Islington is an inner-London borough with a highly competitive school landscape and some of the most oversubscribed schools in the capital. The borough borders Camden and Hackney and is popular with young professional families. Highbury Fields School, Elizabeth Garrett Anderson and Highgate Wood School are among the most popular secondaries. Primary catchments can be extremely tight.
