Schools in Kingston upon Thames, London

Kingston upon Thames has consistently been one of the strongest-performing London boroughs for school outcomes. The borough's primaries and secondaries are heavily oversubscribed, particularly in Kingston town and Surbiton. Admission distances are tight.

What the data says about Kingston upon Thames

81.0% of Kingston upon Thames schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Kingston upon Thames is 1.33, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Kingston upon Thames over the most recent reporting month is 82.98, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Kingston upon Thames's schools is 7.9 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Kingston upon Thames?

Kingston upon Thames has 63 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 41 are primary, 23 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Kingston upon Thames schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 15 schools in Kingston upon Thames (23.8%) are rated Outstanding, and 81.0% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Kingston upon Thames?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Kingston upon Thames's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Kingston upon Thames?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Kingston upon Thames appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Kingston upon Thames-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Kingston upon Thames schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
