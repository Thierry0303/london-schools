Schools in Lambeth, London

Lambeth schools cover Brixton, Streatham, Clapham, Vauxhall and Norwood, with a strong mix of state and faith provision. Several of the borough's secondaries are heavily oversubscribed, and primary applications are particularly competitive in the north of the borough. Appeals success rates in Lambeth track broadly with the inner-London average.

What the data says about Lambeth

81.6% of Lambeth schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Lambeth is 1.29, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Lambeth over the most recent reporting month is 179.18, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Lambeth's schools is 4.67 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Lambeth?

Lambeth has 98 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 69 are primary, 28 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Lambeth schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 21 schools in Lambeth (21.4%) are rated Outstanding, and 81.6% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Lambeth?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Lambeth's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Lambeth?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Lambeth appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Lambeth-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Lambeth schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
