Lambeth is a vibrant south London borough stretching from the South Bank to Streatham, with a diverse and improving school landscape. The borough includes highly regarded schools such as Dunraven School and La Retraite RC Girls' School. Brixton, Clapham and Streatham are popular with young families drawn by relatively affordable housing and good transport links.
