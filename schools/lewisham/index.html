Lewisham is a south-east London borough with a strong community feel and an improving school landscape. The borough has invested significantly in education over the past decade and several schools are now rated Outstanding. Areas such as Blackheath, Forest Hill and Lee are particularly popular with families. Prendergast schools and Haberdashers' Boys' School are among the most sought-after secondaries.
