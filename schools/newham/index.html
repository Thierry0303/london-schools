Newham in east London has undergone remarkable educational improvement over the past two decades and now has one of the highest proportions of Good and Outstanding schools among inner-London boroughs. The borough benefited significantly from Olympic investment and regeneration. Schools such as Brampton Manor Academy – which sends more students to Oxbridge than most independent schools – have national reputations.
