Schools in Newham, London

Newham's schools serve East Ham, Stratford, Plaistow, Forest Gate and the Royal Docks. The borough has been consistently in the top half of London for KS2 and KS4 progress, with several secondaries achieving national recognition. Reception demand is high and admissions distances are short across most of the borough.

What the data says about Newham

92.9% of Newham schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Newham is 1.14, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Newham over the most recent reporting month is 357.29, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Newham's schools is 2.62 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Newham?

Newham has 112 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 79 are primary, 31 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Newham schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 40 schools in Newham (35.7%) are rated Outstanding, and 92.9% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Newham?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Newham's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Newham?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Newham appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Newham-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Newham schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
