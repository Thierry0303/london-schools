Schools in Redbridge, London

Redbridge schools cover Ilford, Wanstead, Woodford and South Woodford, with a notable selective and grammar presence. The borough's primaries are heavily oversubscribed in central and southern wards. Many parents prepare for the 11+ from Year 4 onwards.

Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Redbridge?

Redbridge has 95 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 52 are primary, 15 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Redbridge schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 0 schools in Redbridge (0.0%) are rated Outstanding, and 0.0% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Redbridge?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Redbridge's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Redbridge?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Redbridge appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Redbridge-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Redbridge schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
