Schools in Richmond upon Thames, London

Richmond upon Thames has one of the highest proportions of Outstanding-rated schools in London, particularly in primary phase. The borough covers Richmond, Twickenham, Teddington, Whitton and Hampton. Reception catchment radii are tight across most of the borough.

What the data says about Richmond upon Thames

75.0% of Richmond upon Thames schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Richmond upon Thames is 1.26, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Richmond upon Thames over the most recent reporting month is 170.12, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Richmond upon Thames's schools is 7.95 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Richmond upon Thames?

Richmond upon Thames has 84 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 64 are primary, 27 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Richmond upon Thames schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 23 schools in Richmond upon Thames (27.4%) are rated Outstanding, and 75.0% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Richmond upon Thames?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Richmond upon Thames's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Richmond upon Thames?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Richmond upon Thames appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Richmond upon Thames-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Richmond upon Thames schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
