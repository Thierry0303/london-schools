Richmond upon Thames is consistently rated as one of the best boroughs in London for education and quality of life. The borough has an exceptionally high proportion of Outstanding-rated schools and benefits from low crime, extensive green space and strong community networks. Schools in Richmond, Twickenham and Ham are highly sought after and catchments can be very tight.
