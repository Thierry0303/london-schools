Schools in Southwark, London

Southwark's schools stretch from Bermondsey and Borough up through Camberwell, Peckham and Dulwich. The borough has a mix of community, faith and academy schools, with some of the most competitive admissions arrangements in south-east London. Many parents apply across the Southwark/Lambeth boundary, so understanding catchment is essential.

What the data says about Southwark

85.1% of Southwark schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Southwark is 1.26, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Southwark over the most recent reporting month is 256.36, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Southwark's schools is 4.38 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Southwark?

Southwark has 121 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 78 are primary, 39 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Southwark schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 29 schools in Southwark (24.0%) are rated Outstanding, and 85.1% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Southwark?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Southwark's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Southwark?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Southwark appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Southwark-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Southwark schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
