Schools in Sutton, London

Sutton operates one of London's most selective secondary systems, with a high proportion of grammar provision. The borough's primaries are heavily oversubscribed in central Sutton, Carshalton and Cheam. Many parents begin 11+ preparation in Year 4.

What the data says about Sutton

86.5% of Sutton schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Sutton is 1.25, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Sutton over the most recent reporting month is 156.58, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Sutton's schools is 6.96 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Sutton?

Sutton has 74 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 45 are primary, 27 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Sutton schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 17 schools in Sutton (23.0%) are rated Outstanding, and 86.5% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Sutton?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Sutton's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Sutton?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Sutton appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Sutton-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Sutton schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
