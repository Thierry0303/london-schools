Schools in Tower Hamlets, London

Tower Hamlets schools serve one of London's most diverse boroughs, covering Whitechapel, Bow, Poplar and the Isle of Dogs. The borough has seen substantial improvement in KS2 and KS4 results, and several secondaries rank among the highest performing in the country for disadvantaged-pupil progress. Admissions are coordinated by the local authority.

What the data says about Tower Hamlets

87.4% of Tower Hamlets schools are currently rated Good or Outstanding by Ofsted, compared with a London average of roughly 90% for primary and 80% for secondary. The average number of applications per place across Tower Hamlets is 1.2, a useful indicator of how competitive local admissions are. The average number of recorded crime incidents within 500 metres of a school in Tower Hamlets over the most recent reporting month is 347.93, drawn from the Metropolitan Police open data API. The mean IMD (Index of Multiple Deprivation) decile of Tower Hamlets's schools is 3.03 — 1 is most deprived, 10 is least deprived. Frequently asked

How many schools are there in Tower Hamlets?

Tower Hamlets has 111 schools in our directory, drawn from the Department for Education's Get Information About Schools register. Of these, 77 are primary, 32 are secondary, and the remainder are special, all-through, or post-16.

What proportion of Tower Hamlets schools are Outstanding?

As of the latest Ofsted Management Information release, 28 schools in Tower Hamlets (25.2%) are rated Outstanding, and 87.4% are rated Good or Outstanding. Ratings are refreshed monthly from the Ofsted MI publication.

When do I apply for a Reception or Year 7 place in Tower Hamlets?

Reception applications in England close on 15 January each year for the September intake. Year 7 (secondary transfer) applications close on 31 October. Tower Hamlets's applications are coordinated by the local authority through the eAdmissions system. See our full Reception timeline guide.

How does the school appeals process work in Tower Hamlets?

If you are refused your preferred school place, you have a statutory right of appeal. Tower Hamlets appeals are typically heard in May (Reception) and June–July (secondary). See our appeals tracker for Tower Hamlets-level success rates by year.

How can I tell which Tower Hamlets schools are over-subscribed?

Each school listing on this site shows the number of applications per place from the DfE's published admissions data. Apps-per-place above 2 typically indicates strong over-subscription. We refresh this data annually when the DfE publishes its admissions and offers release.
