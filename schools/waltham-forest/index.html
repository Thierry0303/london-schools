Waltham Forest in north-east London has seen significant improvement in its school quality over the past decade. The borough is increasingly popular with young families priced out of Hackney and Islington, attracted by its improving schools and better value housing. Areas such as Walthamstow, Leytonstone and Chingford offer a range of primary and secondary options.
